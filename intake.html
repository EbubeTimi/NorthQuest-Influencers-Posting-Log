Reaching 0 views is no small thing. Let me show you what being a NorthQuest creator looks like.

Two a day, both platforms

Shoot two videos every day, and post the same two on both TikTok and Instagram. The second one before 11:30 PM.

there, that is 14 a week and 62 a month, our standard monthly count. The more you post, the better your chances. Don't wait for a perfect video, post, learn, post again.

Logging takes seconds

Every day, after you post, drop your TikTok and Instagram links into our tracker. That is how your work is counted. A video not logged the same day may not count, so make it a habit.

4Tap Submit, that's it, you're logged

How to sign your contract

Two documents below, one to read, one to sign. Use Google Docs and Adobe Acrobat.

Document 1, read this first
⬇Open Document 1›
Document 2, the contract to sign
⬇Open Document 2›
  1. Under Parties at the top of Document 2, fill in your name and address.
  2. At the bottom, fill in your name and today's date. Leave the signature line blank, you'll sign it in Adobe Acrobat.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen, then tap the three dots in the top right corner.
  4. Tap Share & export.
  5. Swipe across the app icons that show up, and tap Adobe Acrobat.
  6. In Adobe Acrobat, tap the pen icon at the bottom left, and sign the contract.
  7. Tap the export icon (an arrow out of an open box) and save the signed file to your phone. Name it with just your full name, for example Ada Okeke.
  8. Come back here and upload that file below.
Phones word these menus differently. If you get stuck, message Smith on WhatsApp below and he will walk you through it on your own phone. Do not guess and do not send an unsigned copy.
