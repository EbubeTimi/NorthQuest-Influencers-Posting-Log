How you log your posts

Logging takes seconds

Every day, after you post, you drop your TikTok and Instagram links into our tracker. That is how your work is counted. A video not logged the same day may not count, so make it a habit.

How to sign your contract

Read the steps, watch the quick video, then sign and upload your contract. We are one tap away if you have questions.

Understand the steps
  1. Tap "open and download your contract" below. It opens a Google Drive folder.
  2. Find the document with your name (it may be named "insert your name") and open it.
  3. Fill the TOP of the contract: your name and your address.
  4. Tap the three dots at the top right, choose Share & export, then Send a copy, then PDF.
  5. Choose More, then pick Adobe Acrobat (install it free if you do not have it).
  6. In Acrobat, scroll to the BOTTOM where the signature goes. Tap Fill & Sign, draw your signature, place it, and size it.
  7. Also fill in the date at the bottom.
  8. Tap the upload/share arrow, tap Send a copy, and save it as your name.
  9. Come back here and upload your signed copy below.
