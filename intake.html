Reaching 0 views is no small thing. Let me show you what being a NorthQuest creator looks like.

Two a day, both platforms

Shoot two videos every day, and post the same two on both TikTok and Instagram. The second one before 11:30 PM.

there, that is 14 a week and 62 a month, our standard monthly count. The more you post, the better your chances. Don't wait for a perfect video, post, learn, post again.

Logging takes seconds

Every day, after you post, drop your TikTok and Instagram links into our tracker. That is how your work is counted. A video not logged the same day may not count, so make it a habit.

What it looks like
1Open the tracker link (always in the main group's description)
2Pick your name, it tells you which video you're on
3Paste your TikTok link and your Instagram link
4Tap Submit, that's it, you're logged

How your pay works

Your base pay is set when you join. Each video you log earns its share of that monthly amount. You are paid once a month, on or before the last day.
