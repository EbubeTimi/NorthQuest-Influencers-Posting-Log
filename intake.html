Content guide

Four formats that work

Don't sell the app. Sell the feeling of having your money sorted. These four keep working:

The relatable joke. Laugh at a shared truth. NorthQuest is in the punchline, not the pitch.

The soft flex. Notice the difference between people who save and people who don't. Calmly.

The money drama. A story, a transfer, a twist that stops the scroll.

The casual mention. NorthQuest is just on the list of things you use. No fanfare.

Prefer written steps? Read how to sign
  1. Tap the link to the contracts Drive folder above.
  2. Find the document named "insert your name" and open it. If you have Google Docs on your phone it opens there.
  3. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  4. Choose Share and export.
  5. Tap PDF, then OK.
  6. Tap More, then choose Adobe Acrobat (install it if you do not have it).
  7. In Acrobat, scroll to where your signature goes. At the bottom tap Fill and sign.
  8. If you have no saved signature, tap Add initials.
  9. Draw your signature, then tap Done at the top left.
  10. Tap where the signature should go to place it.
  11. Resize it to your liking.
  12. Tap the upward arrow icon at the top right.
  13. Tap Send a copy.
  14. Save it as your name, then upload it below.
