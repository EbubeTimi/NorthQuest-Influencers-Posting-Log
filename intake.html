How you log your posts

Logging takes seconds

Every day, after you post, you drop your TikTok and Instagram links into our tracker — the same video, both links. That is how your work is counted. A video not logged the same day may not count, so make it a habit.

The tracker link is always in the group description. Check there first — please do not ask in the group where the link is; it never moves. It opens on your phone, you paste your two links, and you are done.

Last step

How to sign your contract

This takes about ten minutes. You will use three things: the contract in Google Drive, the Adobe Acrobat app, and this page. Read to the end before you tap anything.

Read the whole contract, and sign it in two places This is your real agreement with NorthQuest — how you are paid, who owns the accounts, and what happens to your content. Read every line before you sign. When you fill it in, you must complete two places: the TOP (your name and address) and the BOTTOM (your signature, your name and the date). A contract signed at only one end is not valid and gets sent back — this is the single most common mistake, so do both.
Read this first The document in Google Drive is the shared master copy that every creator uses. Never type in it. You are only making your own copy from it, and you fill that copy in Adobe Acrobat. If you type in it by mistake, delete what you wrote as soon as you have your copy.
Before you start
Three apps on your phone
  • Google Drive — this is where the contract lives.
  • Google Docs — the contract opens in this app, and it is the one that lets you save your own copy as a PDF.
  • Adobe Acrobat — this is where you fill the contract in and sign it.
Install all three free from the Play Store or App Store now, before you start. Most people who get stuck halfway are missing one of them.
Every button on this page opens in a new tab, so this page stays open behind it. When your signed copy is ready, switch back to this tab and finish at the bottom. If you prefer to watch it done first, use the video button below.
Part 1 of 3 · in Google Drive, then Google Docs
Make your own copy
  1. Tap “Tap here to open your contract in Google Drive” below — the last button, with the down arrow. Not the video button.
  2. It opens a folder. Open the contract document inside it. It will open in the Google Docs app — that is normal, and it is why you need Google Docs installed.
  3. You are now in Google Docs. Tap the three dots in the top right corner. A menu slides in from the side of your screen.
  4. Tap Share & export, then Save a copy, then choose PDF format and press OK. Your phone prepares the PDF.
  5. A menu comes up from the bottom of your screen showing your apps. Swipe through them to the right, tap More, and open Adobe Acrobat. If you do not have it, install it free from the Play Store or App Store and come back to this step.
Part 2 of 3 · in Adobe Acrobat
Fill both ends — top and bottom
  1. At the TOP of the contract, fill in your name and your address.
  2. Now for your signature at the BOTTOM. Look at the menu bar running along the bottom of Acrobat. On the bottom left, the last icon is a pencil. Tap that pencil — it is the Draw tool. Draw your signature with your finger, then drag it and resize it so it sits inside the signature slot.
  3. Still at the bottom, add your name and the date beside your signature.
  4. Save it, and use your own name as the file name — something like Ada Okeke - Signed Contract. Acrobat saves it onto your phone.
Use the pencil, not the signature tool Acrobat has its own signature tool, and it will ask you to pay for a subscription. You do not need it and we are not asking you to pay for anything. The pencil (Draw) on the bottom left is free and it is the one we use.
Part 3 of 3 · back on this page
Send it to us
  1. Switch back to this tab.
  2. Upload the PDF you just saved, using the upload box below.
  3. Tick the agreement box, type your full name, and check the date.
  4. Tap Submit and finish. That is you done.
Phones word these menus differently. If you cannot find Save a copy, or Adobe Acrobat will not open your file, message Smith on WhatsApp below and he will walk you through it on your own phone. Do not guess and do not send an unsigned copy.
