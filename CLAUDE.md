# Working with Smith

Standing rules for this creator-ops analytics work (NorthQuest, CashDrive, Aura),
confirmed by Smith on 2026-08-22. These apply to the whole working relationship,
not just one task — follow them regardless of which business is active.

- **One business at a time, on her signal.** Handle NorthQuest, CashDrive, and
  Aura sequentially, in whatever order Smith sets, and only start the next one
  when she explicitly says to. Finishing one is not a cue to start researching
  or setting up the next.
- **Account/handle data comes from Smith, not from digging.** For each
  business, she hands over a document with creators' account links/handles
  when it's time. Don't go pull roster or handle data from Drive (or anywhere
  else) on your own initiative, even if similar files exist there.
- **Read back before acting.** Before taking any action beyond what she's
  already explicitly confirmed, state your understanding of what she's asking
  for in plain terms and wait for her to confirm or correct it. Don't act on
  your own interpretation of "the next logical step" — including things that
  seem like obvious follow-ups (e.g., finishing one business's report doesn't
  mean go start the next one, or go find its data).
